Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you order through this website, why, and what your rights are under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number, so we can prepare your order and contact you about it.
  • Your delivery address, only if you choose delivery.
  • The items you order, any notes you add, and the time of the order.
  • A record that you agreed to this policy when you placed the order.

We do not ask for payment card details on this website. We do not create customer accounts. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels.

Why we use it

We use your information only to receive, prepare, deliver and follow up on your order, to deal with any complaint, and to meet legal obligations. We rely on your consent (the box you tick at checkout) and on the need to carry out your order. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

Who sees it

Our staff who handle orders can see it. Our website hosting provider stores it on our behalf. If you tap the button to send your order on WhatsApp, the message goes through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. Links to Instagram and other sites are governed by those sites’ own policies.

How long we keep it

Order records are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Orders are stored on our server and can only be read by someone with the admin password. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive information in the order notes.

Children

This website is not aimed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place the order.

Cookies and storage

See our Cookies Policy.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
