Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Cancelling an order

Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone as soon as possible. If we have not started preparing your order, we will cancel it. Once preparation has started, we may not be able to cancel it.

Problems with your order

If an item is missing, wrong, or there is a problem with the quality of your food, contact us on the same day with your order number (it starts with BW) and, if you can, a photo. We will put it right by remaking the item, replacing it, or refunding the affected item, depending on the situation.

If we cannot fulfil your order

If we have to cancel your order, for example because an item is unavailable, we will tell you. If you have already paid, we will refund you in full.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are made through the same method you used to pay, or another method we agree with you on WhatsApp.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect your rights under Nigerian consumer protection law.
