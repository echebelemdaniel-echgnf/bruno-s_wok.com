Cookies Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Short version: this website does not use cookies, and it does not track you.

What we do not use

  • No cookies of any kind.
  • No analytics, advertising or social media tracking.
  • No third-party embeds, fonts or scripts. Everything on the page is served from our own site.

What we store on your device

To keep your order if you refresh the page, the website saves your basket (the items and quantities) in your browser’s local storage. This is needed for the ordering feature to work, it stays on your device, and it is cleared after you send an order. You can also clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Links to other sites

The WhatsApp and Instagram links take you to those services, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.

Why there is no cookie banner

A consent banner is for cookies and trackers that are not essential. We do not use any. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
