Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Tap Add on the dishes you want, open your order, fill in your details and tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order is recorded and a ready-written message opens in WhatsApp for you to send. Your order is only confirmed once the restaurant replies.

Do you do pickup or delivery?

You can choose either at checkout. If you choose delivery, the restaurant confirms on WhatsApp whether they can deliver to you, and the fee.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged with the restaurant on WhatsApp after your order is received.

Where are you?

#5 Sani Abacha Road, New GRA, Port Harcourt.

What are your opening hours?

Message us on WhatsApp to confirm.

Do the prices change?

The prices shown are the ones the restaurant has set. A dish that is not available shows as Sold out. If unsure, check with us on WhatsApp before paying.

Can I ask a question before ordering?

Yes. Message or call +234 903 235 7438, or send a DM on Instagram @brunos_wok.
